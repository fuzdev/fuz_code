feat: prepared diffs, row identity, and highlighting for the diff viewer - `create_diff_side` lexes a text once into a `DiffSide` reusable across diffs (cache it by content), and `create_diff_model` pairs two sides with their `DiffLine`s, computed by `diff_lines` or supplied by the caller, pairing changes and their emphasis lazily. `render_diff_unified_rows_html` and `render_diff_split_rows_html` render a range of rows from any `DiffRowSource` (the surface `DiffModel` extends), `render_diff_model_unified_html` and `render_diff_model_split_html` the whole document, and `render_diff_unified_html` / `render_diff_split_html` compose them from two strings with unchanged output apart from the new attributes. Every row and cell carries `data-a` / `data-b` line numbers (absent on the side it lacks) and, inside a hunk, `data-hunk`. A `highlight` option (`DiffLineRange`s per side) adds the `diff_highlight` class, styled in `theme_diff.css` from the accent intent (`theme_variables.css` adds `--accent_50`). `DiffRowSource.elided_count` may return `null` for an unknown count, and a gap with no rows renders as a count row. `apply_diff_highlight` toggles that class on rendered rows. `CodeDiff` takes `model` as an alternative to `a` and `b`, a `highlight` prop that changes classes in place without re-rendering, and an async `scroll_to_line(side, line)` method; the preprocessor leaves usages with `highlight` to runtime. `lexer.ts` adds `create_syntax_line_index` and `render_syntax_html_line_range` to render a few lines of a lexed text without the lines before them
